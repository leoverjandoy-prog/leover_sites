A little message, just for you

Welcome to Doy Sites

Make a message personal. Add your words, a photo, or your voice — then share a link protected by a password.

✦ Create your identity

Tell the receiver who this message is from. Your details are included only in the message you create.

Camera access works on HTTPS or localhost. Photo is embedded in the encrypted message, so keep it small.

💌 Write your message

Say what you want to say. Add emojis, a picture, and even a voice recording.

Up to 2,500 characters.

Images are automatically resized/compressed before encryption. Larger media may take longer to upload.

Recording requires microphone permission and a supported browser.

🔐 Lock your message

Choose a password to give only to the person receiving the link. The message is encrypted in your browser before the link is created.

Privacy note: no database means this app cannot revoke a copied link or guarantee deletion from every copy. The expiration is checked when the page opens; a receiver could save content before then. Never use this for sensitive or confidential information.

Send the link to your recipient, then share the password separately. If the link is too long for an app to accept, remove the media and try again.
